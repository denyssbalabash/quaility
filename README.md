# Quaility

Coming-soon site for Quaility, the largest quail egg factory in Europe (Ukraine).

Live: https://denyssbalabash.github.io/quaility/

One static `index.html`: a scroll film of a quail egg on a conveyor sealed into a blister (Three.js), the Quail / Quality / Quaility lockup, AI as Autosexing Inherited, and a digital business card with a vCard QR code.

To fill in the owner's card, edit the `OWNER` object near the top of the script in `index.html`.
